    SPACE ENVIRONMENT EXPLORER

    Welcome aboard.

    Satellite tracks and space weather, in one picture — for the Navy Space Cadre and METOC community, and for anyone curious about the near-Earth environment.

    This is a work in progress. We build it in our own free time, so expect rough edges and things we have not finished yet. If you see something that needs clearing up, please tell us.

    A free, non-commercial personal project. It does not represent the U.S. Navy or the U.S. Government. It teaches; it is not for operational use. The full disclaimer is under About.

    LT Sean Egan, PhD, USN & AG1 Derek Conklin, USN

    ABOUT THIS PROJECT

    We’re Derek and Sean.

    Space Environment Explorer is meant to do two jobs well: give you useful current data when you need it, and help you understand the satellite and space-weather concepts behind what you are seeing.

    CREATORS AG1 Derek Conklin, USN LT Sean Egan, PhD, USN

    We build this together in our own free time — Derek on operations and design, Sean on science and development — for fellow Space Cadre, METOC officers, Aerographer’s Mates, and curious learners.

    The site combines authoritative observations, assimilated products, operational model output, and clearly labeled teaching graphics.
